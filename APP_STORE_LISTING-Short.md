Finds duplicate and near-duplicate photos and helps you delete the extras.

• Scan your Photos library, an album, or a folder
• Exact matches (byte-for-byte) and similar photos (on-device Vision)
• "Select all but first" keeps one copy per group

100% on-device. No account, no ads, no tracking. Mac, iPhone, and iPad.
